# Data Visualization – Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing sales data to identify important business trends, patterns, and performance across different categories, regions, customer segments, products, and time periods.

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Compare sales across product categories.
* Analyze sales by region and customer segment.
* Identify top-performing products and sub-categories.
* Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Analyze sales by shipping mode.
* Visualize the distribution of sales values.
* Extract key business insights from the data.

## Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Google Colab
* Jupyter Notebook
* GitHub

## Visualizations

The project includes the following visualizations:

1. Total Sales by Product Category
2. Total Sales by Region
3. Monthly Sales Trend
4. Total Sales by Customer Segment
5. Top 10 Products by Sales
6. Total Sales by Ship Mode
7. Total Sales by Sub-Category
8. Distribution of Sales
9. Annual Sales Performance
